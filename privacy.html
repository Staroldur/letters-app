Privacy Policy

For parents and guardians using Dino, the Hebrew alphabet-learning game for iPhone and iPad, and its companion website.

At a glance

Dino does not require an account. The game has no advertising or embedded analytics or tracking SDKs. Gameplay runs on the device; the app does not send gameplay activity to a developer-operated server.

Apple handles in-app purchases. If you choose to email support, the developer receives the information you send. Visiting this website also involves its hosting provider, GitHub Pages.

1. Gameplay and on-device storage

The game uses bundled images, words and audio. It does not ask for a child's name, email address, location, contacts, photos or microphone access. There is no in-game account, chat or upload feature.

The app stores a local flag indicating whether the full game is unlocked, together with a development-override flag. The current game code does not save learning progress or a personal player profile. The selected mode and the parent-verification session are held in memory.

The app writes technical messages to the system console, including purchase status, StoreKit responses and errors. The reviewed app code does not upload these logs to the developer. Apple may separately handle diagnostic information according to your device settings and its policies.

2. Purchases through Apple

The full-game unlock and purchase restoration use Apple's StoreKit through the bundled GodotStoreKit2 plugin. The app requests product information and processes purchase and restoration results to determine access to the paid modes.

Information processed by the app can include the product identifier, localized price, purchase status, transaction state, purchase or revocation dates, and error details. The app saves the unlock flag locally; its own storage code does not save a transaction history.

Payment and Apple Account details are handled by Apple. The app does not ask you to enter payment-card details. Apple may provide developers with store reports and diagnostic information under its own policies and your settings. See Apple's App Store & Privacy notice for Apple's handling of purchases and related data.

3. Parent actions and voluntary support

The Parents' Corner requires completion of an arithmetic challenge before its actions are available. Verification is temporary and is cleared when the corner closes or the app loses focus. This gate is intended to involve an adult; it does not collect or verify anyone's identity or age.

  • Share: opens the iOS share sheet with Dino's fixed App Store link. The game does not attach a child's information, gameplay results or files. Your chosen app or service handles what you send under its own policy.
  • Rate and privacy links: open an external App Store page or this website. Those services handle requests according to their policies.
  • Contact: opens your email app with the support address and a subject. Nothing is sent automatically. If you send an email, the developer and email providers process your address, message and any attachments to handle your request.

Parents and guardians should send support requests themselves. Please do not include children's names, photos or other unnecessary personal information. Support correspondence is used to answer the request and resolve related issues.

4. Retention and deletion

The local unlock flag remains in the app's storage until that storage is removed. Deleting the app (rather than offloading it) removes its local app data from that installation. Device backups may retain app data according to your Apple settings; restored backups may bring it back.

Deleting the app does not erase Apple's purchase records or cancel your entitlement. A previous purchase can be restored through Apple. Apple's retention and deletion rules apply to records it holds.

If you have contacted support, you can request access to or deletion of your correspondence by emailing staroldur@gmail.com. The developer may need to verify the request and retain information where necessary to resolve an ongoing issue or meet applicable legal obligations. No fixed automatic deletion period for support email is stated here.

5. This website and external services

These pages do not set cookies or include JavaScript, analytics, tracking pixels, forms or externally hosted fonts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives technical information when serving pages; its documentation states that GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Following an App Store, email or other external link takes you to a separate service. Its privacy policy applies there. The absence of tracking code on these pages does not prevent those services from processing information.

6. Contact and policy changes

For questions about Dino's privacy practices, support correspondence or deletion requests, contact the developer at staroldur@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated when the app or its data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes should be reviewed by a parent or guardian before continuing to use the affected features.
